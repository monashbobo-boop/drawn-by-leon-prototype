Who’s holding the pen?

Hi, I’m Leon. I was an art teacher for over ten years, and now I draw live caricatures and custom portraits from photos.

My style is detailed and charming, with gentle exaggeration that keeps everyone recognisable. Every drawing is done by hand, in black and white or colour. I’m based in Melbourne.

Your face here?

Every caricature starts as a light pencil sketch. Tap the sheet, pick up the marker and colour one of my drawings in yourself.

At your event I do the colouring, of course. Guests sit down, smile, and watch their own portrait come together line by line.

  • 12–15 people an hour in black and white
  • 8–10 people an hour in colour
